Healthcare AI Adoption Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes artificial intelligence adoption in the healthcare industry compared with businesses overall using Ramp AI Index data from November 2024 to October 2026.

Project Objective

I built this project to understand how healthcare organizations are adopting AI, identify adoption trends, investigate potential barriers and strengthen my data analysis, research and data storytelling skills.

Data Source

Ramp AI Index

24 months of monthly AI adoption data

Healthcare and overall business adoption rates

Supporting healthcare industry research

Methodology

Collected historical data using the Ramp Data connector.

Compared healthcare adoption rates against the overall business baseline.

Calculated percentage-point changes and adoption gaps.

Created visualizations to identify long-term trends.

Evaluated competing explanations using independent research.

Presented findings in a professional data story.

Competing Explanations

The project investigated several potential reasons for the persistent adoption gap:

Patient privacy and regulatory requirements.

Clinical safety and technology maturity.

Implementation and integration costs.

AI usage not captured through direct business spending.

The supporting research suggests that multiple factors may influence healthcare AI adoption, but the available data cannot establish causation.

Limitations

Ramp measures observable paid AI adoption rather than actual AI usage. Free tools, bundled AI services and purchases outside Ramp may not be captured. Ramp customers may also not represent all businesses.

Business Recommendations

Healthcare organizations should prioritize secure, cost-effective and clinically validated AI solutions that support operational efficiency, regulatory compliance and patient safety.
